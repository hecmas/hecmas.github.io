---
title: "RapidUp: Multi-Domain Permutation Protocol for Lookup Tables"
authors: [me, Jordi Baylina Melé, Daniel Lubarov, Jose Luis Muñoz Tapia]
date: 2022-08-13
venue: IACR ePrint 2022/1050
type: preprint
abstract: >-
  SNARKs for standard cryptographic primitives often include SNARK-unfriendly operations like XOR. Previous protocols
  like Plookup introduced lookup arguments but were limited to the same circuit. RapidUp solves this by unfolding the
  grand-product polynomial into two equivalent polynomials, with selector-based generalization.
