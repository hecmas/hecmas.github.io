abstract: >-
  The security analysis of ZisK's proof system, in four parts: the proving system (knowledge soundness of every
  argument), the recursive aggregation (an accepting final proof yields a bounded collection of locally satisfying,
  bus-balanced AIR witnesses), the semantics (such a collection encodes a successful execution of the committed
  program; stated, and partly proved), and their composition into the main theorem, with a ledger of its assumptions
  and limitations.
