  An introduction to the Polygon zkEVM as a ZK rollup and to how its prover works: batches are proven with eSTARK,
  recursively compressed and aggregated, and finally wrapped in a fflonK proof that is verified on L1, with real
  prover costs and timings. We then look at the zkEVM as a tailor-made processor (zkASM and PIL), present the
  hackathon tracks, including implementing the Monolith hash function in Circom, and walk through the zkEVM stack
  live.
