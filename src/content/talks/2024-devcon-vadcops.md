abstract: >-
  VADCOP is a proving method using STARKs to achieve cost-efficiency by focusing on active parts of the execution
  trace rather than the entire trace. Traditional modular designs, which divide machines into components and use
  relational arguments, face inefficiencies due to the padding of unused cells with dummy values. VADCOPs optimize
  performance by allowing maximum modularity and avoiding unused components, making proof generation precise and
  efficient without unnecessary redundancy.
