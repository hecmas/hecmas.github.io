### Mathematical Background

- ⚫ [A Computational Introduction to Number Theory and Algebra](https://shoup.net/ntb/) [2008] - Victor Shoup
- 🔵 [Abstract Algebra: Theory and Applications](http://abstract.ups.edu/) [2021] - Thomas W. Judson (This book is updated yearly)

### Cryptography

#### Pure Cryptography

- 🔵 [Handbook of Applied Cryptography](https://cacr.uwaterloo.ca/hac/) [2001] - Menezes
- 🔵 [Understanding Cryptography: A Textbook for Students and Practitioners](https://www.crypto-textbook.com/) [2010] - Christof Paar and Jan Pelzl. Video lectures are available [here](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1usFRN4LCMcfIV7UjHNuQg/videos)
- ⚫ [An Introduction to Mathematical Cryptography](https://www.amazon.es/Introduction-Mathematical-Cryptography-Undergraduate-Mathematics/dp/1441926747) [2010] - Many Authors
- 🔵 [Cryptography Made Simple](https://www.amazon.es/Cryptography-Made-Simple-Information-Security/dp/3319219359) [2015] - Nigel Smart
- 🔵 [A Course in Cryptography](https://www.amazon.es/Course-Cryptography-Applied-Undergraduate-Texts/dp/1470450550) [2019] - Heiko Knospe
- 🔴 [A Graduate Course in Applied Cryptography](http://toc.cryptobook.us/) [2020] - Dan Boneh and Victor Shoup (This book will be updated, it is in version 0.5)
- 🔴 [Real-World Cryptography](https://www.amazon.es/Real-world-Cryptography-David-Wong/dp/1617296716) [2021] - David Wong

#### Applications

- ⚫ [Self-Sovereign Identity: Decentralized digital identity and verifiable credentials](https://www.amazon.es/Self-Sovereign-Identity-Decentralized-verifiable-credentials/dp/1617296597) [2001] - Alex Preukschat and Drummond Reed
- ⚫ [Cryptography Algorithms: A guide to algorithms in blockchain, quantum cryptography, zero-knowledge protocols, and homomorphic encryption](https://www.amazon.com/Next-generation-Cryptography-Algorithms-Explained-implementation-ebook/dp/B093Y11H9Q) [2022] - Massimo Bertaccini

#### Elliptic Curve Cryptography

- 🔴 [Elliptic Curves: Number Theory and Cryptography](https://www.amazon.es/Elliptic-Curves-Cryptography-Mathematics-Applications/dp/1420071467) [2008] - Lawrence Washington
- 🔴 [Pairings for Beginners](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5fdbb09f31d71c1227082339/t/5ff394720493bd28278889c6/1609798774687/PairingsForBeginners.pdf) [2012] - Craig Costello

#### Zero-Knowledge Proofs

- ⚫ [Bringing Zero-Knowledge Proofs of Knowledge to Practice](https://www.amazon.es/Bringing-Zero-Knowledge-Proofs-Knowledge-Practice/dp/383253217X) [2012] - Stephan Krenn
- 🔴 [Proofs, Arguments, and Zero-Knowledge](https://people.cs.georgetown.edu/jthaler/ProofsArgsAndZK.html) [2022] - Justin Thaler (This book is not in final form)
- 🔴 [ZKProof Community Reference v0.3](https://docs.zkproof.org/reference) [2022] - Community

### Blockchain

#### Theory

- 🔵 [Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency Technologies: A Comprehensive Introduction](https://www.amazon.com/-/es/Arvind-Narayanan/dp/0691171696) [2016] - Many Authors
- 🔵 [Mastering Bitcoin: Programming the Open Blockchain](https://github.com/bitcoinbook/bitcoinbook) [2016] - Andreas M. Antonopoulos (second version of the book). The have created a Coursera course [here](https://www.coursera.org/learn/cryptocurrency#syllabus)
- 🔵 [Mastering Ethereum: Building Smart Contracts and Dapps](https://github.com/ethereumbook/ethereumbook) [2018] - Andreas M. Antonopoulos and Gavin Wood (they are working in the second version)
- 🔵 [Mastering Monero: The future of private transactions](https://masteringmonero.com/) [2018] - SerHack

#### Solidity

- 🔴 [Ethernaut](https://ethernaut.openzeppelin.com/) - Learn by hacking smart contracts
- ⚫ [CryptoZombies](https://cryptozombies.io/) - Learn by building simple games

### Computer Science

- 🔵 [Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach](https://www.amazon.es/Computational-Complexity-Modern-Approach-English-ebook/dp/B009019SN0) [2009] - Sanjeev Arora and Boaz Barak
- 🔵 [Introduction to the Theory of Computation](https://www.amazon.es/Introduction-Theory-Computation-Michael-Sipser/dp/113318779X) [2012] - Michael Sipser (Third edition)

### Probability

- 🔵 [A First Course in Probability](http://julio.staff.ipb.ac.id/files/2015/02/Ross_8th_ed_English.pdf) [2013] - Sheldon Ross (Ninth edition)
- 🔵 [Probability Cheat Sheet](https://geoffroycouteau.github.io/assets/pdf/cheat_sheet.pdf) [2022] - Geoffroy Couteau

### Recreational

- 🔵 [The Humble Programmer](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/users/EWD/ewd03xx/EWD340.PDF) [1972] - Edsger Dijkstra
- ⚫ [The Codebreakers: The Comprehensive History of Secret Communication from Ancient Times to the Internet](https://www.amazon.es/Codebreakers-Comprehensive-History-Communication-Internet/dp/0684831309) [1997] - David Kahn
- 🔵 [The Code Book: The Science of Secrecy from Ancient Egypt to Quantum Cryptography](https://www.amazon.es/Code-Book-Science-Secrecy-Cryptography/dp/0385495323) [2000] - Simon Singh
